# Hello Muse
<h3>——web version of original story 我的大前辈离家出走了<br><br>by Tenmei</h3>
<br>
Hello Muse is the web version of an original story, telling stories that happened during a mysterious senior student’s stay in your house.<br><br>
The web version enhances interaction, visual, and hearing experience when revealing the real thoughts and emotions of characters through interactions including scrolling, clicking, hovering and others. The storyline was adapted to enhance comprehensibility and highlight parts that allows interaction. The purpose is to let viewers experience things originally described in text using eyes, ears, and hands.<br><br><br>
